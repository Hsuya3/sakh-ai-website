# SAKH AI

### Building an Independent Personal Artificial Intelligence

SAKH AI is an independent artificial intelligence research and development project focused on building a private, intelligent and multilingual personal AI platform.

Our vision is to develop an AI that can understand its users, communicate naturally, see and interpret the surrounding world, remember useful information and interact with computers and digital environments.

We aim to gradually develop our own AI technologies, models and infrastructure while maintaining privacy, reliability and user control.

## Our Vision

We envision SAKH as more than a conventional chatbot.

SAKH is being designed as an integrated personal AI platform combining multiple areas of artificial intelligence.

## Core Development Areas

### 1. AI Intelligence and Reasoning
Developing an intelligent system capable of understanding requests, reasoning through tasks, planning and making informed decisions.

### 2. Multilingual Intelligence
Developing natural communication capabilities across:
- Nepali
- Romanized Nepali
- English
- Dutch
- Other languages

### 3. Voice Intelligence
Building natural voice interaction, multilingual speech recognition, speech generation and voice-controlled computer assistance.

### 4. Visual Intelligence
Developing real computer vision capabilities, including:
- Object recognition
- Scene understanding
- Visual question answering
- Document understanding
- Real-time visual perception

### 5. Personal AI Memory
Building private, personalized memory systems that allow SAKH to remember relevant information and adapt to individual users.

### 6. Computer Automation
Developing the ability to interact with computer applications, manage files and execute tasks through natural-language instructions.

### 7. Independent AI Development
Researching open-weight models, fine-tuning, training infrastructure and eventually proprietary SAKH models.

### 8. AI Wearable Technology
Exploring standalone AI-powered wearable devices that integrate voice, vision, computing and personal intelligence.

## Our Principles

- Privacy-first AI.
- Local processing wherever practical.
- User-controlled personal memory.
- Multilingual accessibility.
- Reliable and safe automation.
- Modular and extensible architecture.
- Long-term independent AI research.

## Development Status

SAKH AI is an active research and development project.

Its capabilities are being developed and tested progressively. Features described in this repository represent project objectives and development directions, not necessarily completed functionality.

## Long-Term Goal

Our long-term goal is to develop an independent personal AI ecosystem combining intelligence, voice, vision, memory and real-world interaction.
